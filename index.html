Most small businesses do not need a big agency process just to fix a website, clean up a workflow, make better use of AI, or build a simple tool. You may not even know what the real problem is yet. That's okay. We can find it together, sort through the options, and help get it handled.

Why I started Audo.

Hi, I'm Aaron. Audo is my independent consulting practice, built for individuals and small businesses that want hands-on technology help from the person actually doing the work.

I have spent 30 years solving technology problems, from building websites in high school to leading product and engineering teams across Cox Automotive, Dealertrack, and Dealer.com. Through prior roles, OEM programs, and agency work, I have also supported M&A, product due diligence, integrations, migrations, launches, and client work for organizations including John Maxwell Leadership Foundation, Hattori Hanzo, L'Oreal, and others.

I started Audo because the best technology help should feel clear, personal, and based on how your business actually works. You should not have to translate your problem into technical language, repeat yourself to a chain of people, or wonder whether anyone understands what is going on.

Bring me the messy version: the website that needs attention, the workflow that keeps wasting time, the AI idea you are unsure about, or the feeling that something could work better. You do not have to diagnose it first. I will help you make sense of it and move forward with care.
