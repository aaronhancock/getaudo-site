Overview

Audo is Aaron Hancock's independent technology consulting practice. This Privacy Policy applies when you visit getaudo.com, browse its service pages, manage cookie preferences, or submit a discovery request.

By using the site, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree, please avoid submitting personal information through the site.

Information Audo collects

Information you provide

When you request a discovery call, Audo may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • An optional business website or other useful link.
  • The service page or general help context and your description of what needs attention.
  • An optional promo code.
  • Your selected appointment time and the contact details you confirm on Google Calendar's booking page.
  • Information you later choose to provide by email, during a call, or while working with Audo.

Information collected automatically

The site and its infrastructure may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, referring page, pages requested, timestamps, and security or delivery logs. When a discovery form is submitted, Audo also records the user agent, referrer, and IP address associated with the request.

Google reCAPTCHA may return risk and verification information such as a score, action, and hostname to help determine whether a request is legitimate.

How information is used

Audo uses information to:

  • Review and respond to discovery requests.
  • Research the issue you describe and prepare for a conversation.
  • Schedule calls and communicate about potential or active consulting work.
  • Provide, support, improve, and document consulting services.
  • Protect the site and forms from spam, abuse, and security threats.
  • Understand site usage and improve content when analytics consent has been given.
  • Maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers and sharing

Audo does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by providers that help operate the site and consulting practice, including:

  • Cloudflare for site delivery, traffic routing, security, and operational insights.
  • Google reCAPTCHA for spam and abuse protection on discovery forms.
  • Google Analytics for optional site analytics after consent.
  • Google Calendar and Google Meet to show live availability, reserve discovery calls, send booking communications, and host video meetings.
  • Email and infrastructure providers used to deliver, store, and respond to discovery requests.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. You can review the Google Privacy Policy, Google Terms of Service, and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Audo may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or support a business transition such as a reorganization or transfer of the practice.

Cookies, browser storage, and analytics

The site stores your cookie choice in your browser under audo_cookie_choice. This is necessary to remember whether you accepted analytics or selected necessary-only operation.

  • Necessary only: Audo does not intentionally load Google Analytics. Site delivery, security, and reCAPTCHA may still process technical information when required to provide the site or protect a submitted form.
  • Accept: Google Analytics is allowed to load and may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and interactions.

After you submit a discovery request, the confirmation page offers a Google Calendar booking page. The Google Calendar frame is not loaded until you choose to show available times. You can instead open the Google booking page in a separate tab.

You can change your choice at any time. Updating the preference controls future analytics loading in this browser; you may also clear existing cookies through your browser settings.

Retention and security

Discovery requests are stored in Audo's server-side database and emailed to Audo so they can be reviewed and answered. Audo keeps inquiry, email, and client records for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the relationship, maintain business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal obligations. Retention can vary based on the nature of the request or engagement.

Audo uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and the site. No online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy rights

You may choose not to provide optional form information or not to submit a discovery request. You can select necessary-only operation or change your cookie preference using the control above.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or limit certain processing. To make a request, email Audo using the contact information below. Audo may need to verify your identity and may retain information when permitted or required by law.

The site is intended for adults and business users. Audo does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this site.
