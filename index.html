Why Audo.

A senior technology partner with real consulting, product, engineering, agency, and AI experience.

Audo is the consulting practice of Aaron Hancock, a product and engineering leader with nearly 30 years in technology. Aaron began building websites in high school, then spent 15+ years across Cox Automotive, Dealertrack, and Dealer.com leading globally distributed teams through M&A processes, product due diligence, acquisitions, integrations, migrations, customer transitions, and major launches, including OEM program work tied to Ford, Lincoln, Toyota, Acura, Honda, and Audi. After much of his automotive career, he moved into large-agency client work, delivering innovative solutions for clients like John Maxwell Leadership Foundation, Hattori Hanzo, L'Oreal, and others.

AI is powerful when it has the right context.

Aaron leaned into AI early because he saw how quickly it could move an idea from a conversation to something people could react to. His teams started turning concepts into working screens in hours instead of weeks, which changed how he thinks about iteration.

But AI is not a silver bullet. The hard part is the business context: customers, priorities, constraints, what happened yesterday, and what needs to happen next. Audo helps clients decide when AI is the right tool, how to use it safely, and how to build prompts, workflows, training, and guardrails that make the work feel clearer instead of riskier.

What that means for you.

  • You get senior judgment without an agency handoff chain.
  • Your problem is evaluated from business, product, software, UX, automation, and AI angles.
  • The same person who helps define the work can also build, explain, and support it.
  • Your budget goes toward useful thinking and execution instead of layers of process.
  • You get practical recommendations, including when not to use AI or build something custom.
  • You work with someone who keeps the context as your tools, priorities, and business evolve.

That is the Audo model: one experienced partner, clear next steps, and support that keeps the context.
